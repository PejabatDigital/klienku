KlienKu

Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

KlienKu is a simple app for keeping track of your leads. It's made by Kasah Kod in Malaysia. This page explains, in plain words, what KlienKu keeps and why.

Your leads live in your Google Sheet

KlienKu shows and changes the leads in a Google Sheet you own. You shared that sheet with KlienKu's Google service account, so the app can read it and write to it. The sheet stays in your Google account, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. KlienKu doesn't keep its own copy of your leads on its servers.

What KlienKu keeps

  • Your email address, and your name and phone number if you add them.
  • Your password, scrambled. We never see or store it as you typed it.
  • The notes you add to your leads, with the date and time, and which lead they belong to. Notes are kept in KlienKu, not in the Google Sheet.
  • Leads, history and settings you added in KlienKu's earlier version (before 10 November 2026). They're no longer shown, and are removed when you delete your account.
  • A sign-in record (the date, your browser and its internet address), so you stay signed in.
  • For a short time, how many times an internet address tried to sign in, so we can stop password guessing.

We only use this to run KlienKu for you.

Your leads' details

You decide what to write about your leads, and you can change or delete it at any time. Since you collect it for your business, it's up to you to follow the law that applies to you, such as Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010. In practice, keep only what you need to follow up. We never contact your leads, and we never use, sell or share their details.

Where it's kept

KlienKu's own data is on Cloudflare's servers, in their Asia-Pacific region. Your phone and computer also keep a copy of your lead list and notes, so KlienKu opens quickly and works offline.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare runs the app and stores KlienKu's data.
  • Google keeps your Google Sheet. KlienKu reads and writes it through Google's Sheets service.
  • Resend sends account emails only: the link to confirm your email and the link to reset your password. It sees only your email address and that email.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no marketing emails. We never sell or share your information. KlienKu uses one cookie, only to keep you signed in.

Backups

Cloudflare automatically keeps restore points of KlienKu's database for 7 days, so your notes can be recovered if something goes wrong. Google keeps your sheet's version history. You can also download your own copy of your leads and notes at any time: Settings → Data → Download leads and notes.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Account → Delete my account. This removes your account and your notes straight away, and clears the device you do it on. Copies in the restore points expire within 7 days. Your Google Sheet isn't touched: delete it in Google Drive if you want it gone. You can also delete one lead at a time (Edit → Delete lead): this removes its row from the sheet and its notes. Or email us from the address you sign in with and we'll delete your account within 30 days.

Signing out removes your lead list from that device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is big, we'll tell you in the app.
