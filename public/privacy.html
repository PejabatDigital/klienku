Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

KlienKu is a simple app that reminds you who to follow up with. It's made by Kasah Kod in Malaysia. This page explains, in plain words, what KlienKu keeps and why.

What we keep

  • Your email address.
  • Your password, scrambled. We never see or store it as you typed it.
  • The leads you add: their names, phone numbers, location, project type, where the enquiry came from, estimated budget, notes and history.
  • Your settings, such as your reminder times and time zone.
  • For reminders, an address for each device you turn them on for. Apple, Google or your browser makes this address so we can send that device notifications.
  • A sign-in record (the date, your browser and its internet address), so you stay signed in.
  • For a short time, how many times an internet address tried to sign in, so we can stop password guessing.

We only use this to run KlienKu for you.

Your leads' details

You decide what to add about your leads, and you can change or delete it at any time. Since you collect it for your business, it's up to you to follow the law that applies to you, such as Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010. In practice, keep only what you need to follow up. We store it for you only. We never contact your leads, and we never use, sell or share their details.

Where it's kept

On Cloudflare's servers, in their Asia-Pacific region. Your phone and computer also keep a copy of your lead list, so KlienKu opens quickly and works offline.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare runs the app and stores the data.
  • Resend sends account emails only: the link to confirm your email and the link to reset your password. It sees only your email address and that email.
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla deliver reminders to your devices. Reminders are encrypted on the way, so they can't read them. Reminders show lead names on your screen. You can change what your lock screen shows in your phone's notification settings.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no marketing emails. We never sell or share your information. KlienKu uses one cookie, only to keep you signed in.

Backups

Cloudflare automatically keeps restore points of the database for 7 days, so your data can be recovered if something goes wrong. You can also download your own copy at any time: Settings → Download my data.

Deleting your data

Open Settings, then Delete my account. This removes your account and all your leads straight away, and clears the device you do it on. Copies in the restore points expire within 7 days. You can also delete one lead at a time (Edit → Delete lead), or email us from the address you sign in with and we'll delete your account within 30 days.

Signing out removes your lead list from that device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is big, we'll tell you in the app.
